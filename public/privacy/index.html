Clima Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Clima is a weather app. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics, and the developer does not run servers that receive your data. The app talks directly to public weather services from your device.

Location

If you allow it, Clima reads your device's location (precise or approximate, as you choose) each time it opens, so it can show the weather where you are. Your coordinates are sent to the services below only to get weather and a place name for that point. Clima does not track your location in the background.

Services that receive requests

  • Open-Meteo — forecasts, air quality, city search, and past model forecasts (coordinates).
  • BigDataCloud — the name of your town from your coordinates.
  • US National Weather Service — US weather alerts and the nearest weather station (coordinates, US only).
  • Iowa Environmental Mesonet — readings from the nearest public airport weather station (station ID).
  • LibreWXR, Esri, NASA GIBS — radar, satellite, and map tiles for the area on screen.
  • Google Fonts — the app's typeface.

Like any website, these services receive your IP address and standard request details and handle them under their own privacy policies. Clima does not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Stored on your device

Your settings, saved cities, last viewed place, "Teach Clima" feedback, and the forecast model Clima trains for your area are kept in the app's local storage on your device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Children

Clima is a general-audience weather app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/ActOfRod/Clima/issues.
